Our club

Rugby for the Piedmont Triad

Triad Rugby brings players together from across Greensboro, Winston-Salem, High Point, and the surrounding communities.

We compete hard, support one another, and welcome players from every experience level. The club is a place to learn the game, challenge yourself, and become part of a team.

Never played rugby?

Getting started

New to rugby?

1

Show up

Contact the club and come watch or join a practice. No previous rugby experience is required.

2

Learn the basics

We will introduce passing, tackling technique, positioning, laws, and team structure at a manageable pace.

3

Become part of the club

Train consistently, develop your skills, and work toward match selection when you are ready.
